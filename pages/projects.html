  • No Scroll Recipes

    An ad-free recipe site. Tap a dish and get the ingredients and steps, with no life stories and no pop-ups.

  • HUSH

    A procedurally generated pixel-art horror game in the browser. Find the sigils, keep your lamp low, and stay quiet.

    Play Source

  • Lanternreach

    A space game in the browser. Trade, fight and explore across a generated galaxy of sixty star systems.

    Play Source

  • Chronoscope: Wayfarer

    An action RPG on top of a simulated 3,000-year history. Change what happens, then travel through time to see the result.

    Play Source

  • The Same Morning

    A branching life simulation where every life starts on the same morning, and what you remember carries into the next one.

    Play Source

  • reversible

    A prototype for building software from intent instead of code, where every action an agent takes can be undone.

    Parametric 3D models in Python, each checked against my printer's limits before it's committed so it prints the first time.
